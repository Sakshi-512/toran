# Pune audit (small, non-random sample)

A hand count of how 10 small businesses in Pune show up online, done to understand the starting
point for Toran's users. It is a small, non-random sample, so it shows direction, not a population
estimate. One of the 10 results was a sponsored ad.

| Measure | Count (of 10) |
| --- | --- |
| No website | 5 |
| Only a free builder page | 3 |
| Their own domain | 2 |
| List a phone number | 9 |

The hand-counted details for each business (how it was found, what was checked, and the date) will
be listed here once they are supplied. No owner names, phone numbers or addresses will be added.

# Customer checklists

Each list holds the questions a customer wants answered before ordering or booking. The agent checks whether each question is answered on the site. Results are marked AI-assessed.

## Bakery

- What do you make, and what does each item cost?
- Do you make eggless options?
- What sizes or quantities can I order?
- How many days ahead do I need to order?
- Do you deliver to my area, and is there a charge?
- Can I order a custom design?
- How do I order and pay?

## Tailoring

- What do you stitch, and what does each item cost?
- What measurements do you need, or can I give a sample piece?
- How many days does stitching take?
- Do you do alterations, and for how much?
- Do you pick up and deliver?
- How do I order and pay?

## Tuition

- Which subjects and classes do you teach?
- What are the fees?
- What are the batch timings?
- Is it online, in person, or both, and where?
- Is there a trial class?
- Who teaches, and what experience do they have?
- How do I enroll and pay?

## Mehendi

- What styles do you do, and what does each cost?
- Can I see photos of past work?
- Do you visit homes, and in which areas?
- How far ahead do I need to book?
- How long does it take to apply?
- How do I book and pay?

## Beauty parlour

- What services do you offer, and what does each cost?
- Do I need an appointment, or can I walk in?
- Do you do home visits, and in which areas?
- What are your days and timings?
- How long does each service take?
- Do you offer bridal packages, and is there a trial?
- How do I book and pay?

## General (any other business)

- What do you sell or offer?
- What does it cost?
- How do I order or book?
- Where do you deliver or serve?
- How do I contact you?
- When are you available?
